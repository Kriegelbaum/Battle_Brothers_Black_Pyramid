Ring Reference

Rings woven with random magical properties

Requires Reforged + Hardened + Black Pyramid
No two rings are alike. Every ring in this mod is forged with a handful of magical properties — drawn at random from a deep pool and rolled in strength — so a ring you find on one campaign will never be the ring you find on the next. A ring's rarity decides how many properties it carries and how potent they may be. Most rings turn up unappraised: blank bands whose powers stay hidden until an Appraisal Scroll lays them bare. Decide for each brother which ring suits his role — and whether a mystery band is worth the gamble of a scroll.
Magic — modest, single-purpose rings

The Three Rarities

Rarity is fixed the moment a ring is created — even a blank band already knows what it will become.

Magic Ring Magic

A simple enchantment with 1–2 lesser properties — resolve, stamina, health or initiative. The everyday ring: a small, reliable edge for any brother.

Appears most often in the alchemist's stock (76% of blank rings) and is the commonest spoil of a fallen champion.

Rare Ring Rare

Carries 2 lesser properties rolled high, and a 70% chance of a third, greater property — a combat skill, physical armour or vision.

Roughly 18% of the alchemist's blank rings. A worthy prize that rewards appraisal.

Legendary Ring Legendary

A treasure of 3–5 properties — lesser and greater enchantments crowned by one mythic property: head-hunting accuracy, raw damage, an extra Action Point and more.

A 1-in-4 legendary instead forgoes its mythic property to grant +1 Reach — a ring that lengthens the arm itself. Only 6% of shop blanks; a rare drop indeed.

Appraisal & Discovery

A ring's powers mean nothing while they remain a secret. Reveal them — once and forever.

Unappraised Ring Unknown Properties

A band bearing a cryptic engraving. Its true properties are hidden, and it shows only a flat value — you cannot tell a worthless trinket from a legendary heirloom by sight alone.

Equip it on a brother in the accessory slot, then use an Appraisal Scroll to unlock its fate.

Appraisal Scroll Consumable · 100g

A manuscript inscribed with a revealing spell. With an unappraised ring worn in a brother's accessory slot, use the scroll — the ring's properties, name and rarity colour are revealed permanently.

The scroll is spent in the casting. Sold by the alchemist alongside the blank rings.

Where Rings Are Found

Three roads lead to a ring — the merchant's table, the corpse of a common foe, and the body of a fallen champion.

The Alchemist's Shop Reliable Supply

Every alchemist keeps 2–4 blank rings and 2–4 Appraisal Scrolls in stock. Blank rarity follows the merchant table — 76% Magic, 18% Rare, 6% Legendary.

Battlefield Loot 0.4% per kill

Any slain enemy may drop a blank ring — a 0.4% chance on every kill, the same for the lowliest thug or the toughest brute. Over a long campaign these add up to a steady trickle of rings to appraise.

Loot-table rarity leans common: 70% Magic, 28% Rare, 2% Legendary.

Boss & Champion Spoils Already Appraised

Bosses and champions drop rings that are already appraised — their powers visible at once, no scroll required. The ring falls to the tile beneath the body, like a trophy claimed in battle.

Per kill: 30% for a Magic ring, 6.25% Rare, 2.08% Legendary.

Rare beasts of the Black Pyramid. The legendary beasts — direwolf, spider, nachzehrer, mummy, serpent and lindwurm — are a special case: each yields one already-appraised ring every time it is slain. A certain spoil, not a chance one. Each beast keeps its own table of fortune, so the ring's rarity depends on which beast fell:

Direwolf, Spider & Nachzehrer: 25% Magic, 60% Rare, 15% Legendary.

Mummy: 15% Magic, 65% Rare, 20% Legendary.

Serpent & Lindwurm: 20% Magic, 55% Rare, 25% Legendary.

A Ring's Name

Once appraised, a ring earns a name drawn from its strongest property — no two read quite the same.

A single-property ring takes a plain title — Ring of the Lion, Ring of Endurance. A ring of several properties earns an adjective from its mightiest enchantment — Deadly Ring of the Reaper, Tempered Ring of the Bulwark, Far-sighted Ring of Owl Eyes. The name is rolled from a wide table of synonyms and beast-words, so even two rings sharing the same property rarely share the same name.
