Warhorses: changelog, versions 0.1.3 – 0.2.5

Original mod · mod author: krigun

A horse is now a roleplay choice for a fighter, not a free bonus. In the open field a rider is fast, and on a good horse he is also hard to unseat. In forests and on rocks, though, he is slower than a man on foot. A rider can now be thrown off his horse by a blow from a glaive or a hook, and an archer in the saddle shoots a little worse. There is a reason to dismount now, and a horse with Pathfinder has become truly valuable — it is no longer handed out to every horse.

All changes are listed below.

Rider: Initiative, Defense, Armor Weight
♘
Rider InitiativeReworked0.1.4
BeforeWhen mounted, a fighter took the horse’s whole Initiative: 150 plus the riding perk bonuses. His own Initiative was ignored, so a rider had no reason to train it.
NowThe rider keeps his own Initiative: base, armor weight, fatigue, his perks and effects. With Advanced Riding he also gets 10% of the horse’s Initiative.
♘
Defense: Horse and Rider SeparatelyReworked0.1.4
BeforeThe rider’s defense was the horse’s defense (plus +30% of the fighter’s base defense with Advanced Riding). Sitting on a horse was a bad deal for a heavy knight because his defense was cut so hard.
NowBefore the hit roll, the game decides who takes the attack:
  • the horse — with a chance equal to its “size” (50–60%); the roll is made against the horse’s defense and the horse takes the damage;
  • the rider — the roll is made against his own defense: 35% of it, 85% with Advanced Riding, with all bonuses included.

The hit chance preview shows an honest average: the defense weighted by the chance to hit the horse.

♘
Armor WeightReworked0.1.4
BeforeThe rider’s body armor and helmet weighed on the horse, lowering its Initiative and Stamina.
NowThe rider’s armor weighs on the rider himself, as it should. The horse carries only its own armor.
Riding Perk Tree
♘
RidingReworked0.1.7
BeforeA mounted rider’s Initiative was equal to the horse’s Initiative; the rider’s own Initiative was ignored.
NowA mounted rider’s Initiative is the rider’s own Initiative plus the bonuses of the riding perks. The perk tooltip now says this directly and also explains falling off a horse and movement costs in the saddle.

Advanced RidingReworked0.1.4 · 0.2.2
Before
  • +30% of the fighter’s base defense added to the horse’s
  • +20 Initiative
  • +1 Reach
  • +10 Breakthrough
  • +10 chance to stay in the saddle
Now
  • attacks on the rider meet 85% of his own defense (35% without the perk)
  • +10% of the horse’s Initiative added to the rider’s
  • chance to fall reduced by 25% of the rider’s current Stamina (up to −75%): a fresh rider holds on better than a tired one
  • +10 Breakthrough — unchanged

Horseback ArcheryReworked0.1.5 · 0.2.5
BeforeWithout the perk −30 Ranged Skill; the perk removed the penalty completely.
NowWithout the perk −35% Ranged Skill, with the perk −15%, and −10% with a crossbow. You can’t shoot from the saddle with no penalty at all — unless you are a Scythian warrior :)

Before+20 Initiative for the rider; +10 Breakthrough for Horse Charge (mistakenly described as “chance to knock the target back”).
Now+40 Initiative for the horse, not the rider. With Advanced Riding the rider gets his share of it. +10 Breakthrough stays, and the description is fixed: higher Line Break Chance and faster acceleration.

Before+20 Initiative when mounted.

Shooting from the Saddle and Height
♘
High Ground BonusNew0.1.5 · 0.1.6
BeforeThe rider used the normal height rules: +10% to hit from above, −10% per level when shooting uphill.
Now
  • shooting down — no bonus to hit, but range from high ground still increases;
  • shooting up — the horse’s height cancels one level of the penalty: one level up has no penalty, two levels −10%, three levels −20%.
Falling off a Horse
♘
New Falling MechanicReworked0.2.0 – 0.2.2
BeforeChecked only when a single hit took 40%+ of Hitpoints. The chance to stay in the saddle was the rider’s Resolve (+10 with Advanced Riding); when the horse was hit, the horse’s Resolve. Weak hits never unseated anyone, and a rider with high Resolve almost never fell.
NowThe check is made on every hit on the rider, with no threshold:
  • 1% for every 2% of Hitpoints lost to the hit;
  • × the attacker’s weapon;
  • + special attack bonus;
  • − Advanced Riding;
  • − the horse’s steadiness. The chance never goes below zero.
Attacker’s weaponMultiplier
Hammers, maces, polearms, flails×1.2
Swords, daggers×0.7
Everything else, ranged and throwing×1

Hooks: polearm Hook, Serpent Hook, Flesh Pull+20%

Ranged attacks unseat riders just as melee ones do. If the horse took the hit, the damage does not count — only the special attack bonus does. Hooks and knockbacks, which used to simply fail against a rider, can now pull him out of the saddle.

Horse SteadinessNew0.2.0

20% of horses get 0 to 20 points of steadiness when created, subtracted from the rider’s chance to fall. Shown in the horse’s tooltip (“Steady”) and in its panel; it is saved and travels with the horse.

Mounted Movement
♘
Movement Cost by TerrainReworked0.2.3 – 0.2.5
BeforeEvery horse had Pathfinder, so a rider always moved by its cost table. A horse without Pathfinder would have moved like a man on foot — no speed at all.
NowOn roads and open fields a horse is twice as fast as a man on foot. On rough terrain an ordinary horse is 1 AP worse than on foot. The rare Pathfinder makes it as good as on foot there and lets it climb for free.
Terrain, AP per tileOn footOrdinary horseHorse with Pathfinder
Road, open field211
Sand222
Rough ground, forest343

Why

A horse gives speed in open country and bogs down in forests and on rocks — there it pays to dismount. A horse that moves through a forest like a man on foot is a rare find.

Compatibility and Interface
☩
Monk and HorseNew0.1.3

A brother with Monastery Training (Black Pyramid) cannot mount a horse. The two trees lock each other: taking Monastery Training closes the riding tree; taking Riding closes the monk tree. Enemy monks never appear on horseback.

Mount / Unmount SkillsFixed0.1.3
BeforeMount was active even with no horse nearby. Disabled skills showed as a solid black icon.
NowMount is active only next to a free horse; otherwise it is greyed out with a tooltip. Both skills have proper grey disabled icons.
