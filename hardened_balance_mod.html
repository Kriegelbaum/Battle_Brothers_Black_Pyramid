Hardened Balance

Skill & system rebalance for Hardened

Requires Hardened + Reforged  ·  pairs with Black Pyramid, Legacy, More Heads
Hardened Balance is a further evolution of an already-excellent mod. Hardened is its own evolutionary branch of Battle Brothers, and the author — after trying many other modifications — chose it for his campaigns. This sub-mod nudges several skills and systems back toward their Reforged roots, or rebalances them outright, so that a long campaign stays both fair and survivable. Every change below lists what it does and why, along with the file it touches.

Campaign Difficulty
500% nerf to 320% difficulty

The standard 500% setting is simply too steep: by day 300+ the "stationary" camps become impassable. Lowering the difficulty to 320% keeps the mod playable for a genuine long-haul run.

Why

Camps can be cleared again — though rarely without losses in your company.

Reworked & Restored Perks
⚔️
Rebuke Reworked

A "cheese" player skill that used to riposte every incoming blow, always, at no Fatigue cost — which made not taking it on a two-handed fighter simply foolish. It was a must-have for everyone; now it's an actual choice.

Brought back in line with the Reforged original: trigger chance is now 35% without a shield (+15% with one) — 10% higher than Reforged's 25%, which felt far too rare for two-handers. The riposte now costs Fatigue equal to the weapon's basic attack (the undead and skeletons, who track no Fatigue, are exempt), requires a successful Morale check (low morale → no riposte), and deals full 100% damage as in the original.

Why

It removes the "free, always-on" cheese while keeping Rebuke worth taking — even for two-handers. Spending Fatigue and passing a morale check is fair to the game's own logic.

Bearded Blade Reworked

This axe skill (which deals no damage and applies Disarmed on a hit) now carries a −15% hit-chance penalty.

Why

Wrenching a weapon out of an opponent's hands should be harder than simply landing an axe blow. The penalty also curbs enemy "spam" of this skill in top-tier fights against humans.

🦴
Bone Breaker Restored · Reforged

Reverted to the Reforged version: an injury is inflicted only after at least 5 HP of damage punches through.

Why

As shipped, this was the undead's ultimate weapon. Ever watched a pair of zombie heroes with one- or two-handed maces — buffed by 2–3 necromancers — cripple your best front-liners in a single turn? That. The 5-HP threshold reins it in.

❤️
Colossus Restored · Reforged

Restored the old Reforged mechanic — the perk grants +25% maximum Hitpoints, as in the original, instead of a flat +15 HP.

Why

Nimble builds are already weak next to the rest; there's no reason to nerf them any further.

🛡️
Man of Steel Restored · Reforged

Restored the Reforged behaviour: armor-penetrating ("through-armor") damage is reduced from the durability of the armor on the body part that was actually hit — roughly Armor ÷ 10 (capped at 100%), tracked separately for head and body. A 350–400 armor piece therefore shaves off ~35–40% of through damage.

In Hardened this was tied to item weight (the Fatigue penalty) rather than durability — a different and usually much smaller number, taken as the minimum of helmet and body weight regardless of where the hit landed (so, in practice, almost always the helmet). A 15-weight helm + 40-weight body would yield ~15% instead of Reforged's 35–40%.

Why

That made heavy infantry — already hard to find and raise, easy to lose — a poor choice next to medium armor. You buy a heavy bro, grind him to level 11 without a single reload… then one goblin with a knife, a crossbowman's double shot, or a couple of Unhold ends him. Restoring the old version gives heavy fighters a real (if not guaranteed) chance to survive; for a 300/300 knight, cutting 30% of through damage is meaningful.

⚡
Unstoppable Reworked

Fixed so the perk's stacks are no longer wasted by crossbow / handgonne reloads or pole-flail wind-ups — these now count as the non-attack actions they are (sprint and passing step don't strip stacks either).

Why

Previously Unstoppable was useless with exactly the weapons that need it most: reloading or spinning up your weapon stripped your stacks before you could act.

Weapon Master Reworked

The random weapon branch granted by Weapon Master can no longer roll a ranged tree (bow, crossbow, throwing).

Why

Otherwise a sword master could "ruin" the perk on a weapon family he will never touch.

Goblin "Anticipation" Reverted

Perk Anticipation removed from goblin ambusher low, goblin fighter low, goblin fighter and goblin shaman. A "stuffy" game-design decision — come on, it's a goblin, it shouldn't be that difficult—more difficult than an orc! (But they're still dangerous, mind you — try getting surrounded by goblins backed by shamans.)

🎖️
Dismissed-Veteran XP Disabled

The routine that redistributes the experience of dismissed veterans is turned off, because this sub-mod assumes you also run Legacy, which adds a similar but expanded version.

Why

Leaving both active would grant double XP on every dismissal, which looks strange.

✨
Extra Perk Every 4 Levels Restored · Reforged

Restored the original Reforged mechanic: brothers earn an additional perk point every 4 levels past level 11.

Why

Without it, a bro is "finished" at level 11 — what he is, is all he'll ever be ("let him live to a hundred"). The old curve keeps you scouting fresh recruits and stops old, crippled veterans from becoming pure dead weight — otherwise it's simply more profitable to dismiss them.

🏛️
Training-Hall Skill Purchase Removed

Removed the option to buy skills at the town Training Hall.

Why

Purchasable skills flattened the individuality and uniqueness of each brother.
